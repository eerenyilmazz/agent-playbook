# Tax, Returns and Compliance

> Draft, not legal or tax advice. Every rule must be confirmed by an expert.

## Tax and invoices
- Tax rates and categories are configuration, never hard-coded.
- Consumer prices shown tax-inclusive; breakdown stored per line.
- e-Invoice / e-Archive via an approved integrator (Turkey); confirm obligations.

## Returns and withdrawal
- Distance-sales rules apply (TR: Mesafeli Sözleşmeler Yönetmeliği). Confirm current withdrawal period and exceptions.
- Return reason, dates, refund amount recorded and linked to the order.

## Required texts
Distance sales contract, preliminary information form, return policy, privacy notice, cookie policy, terms of use, seller identity/contact info.

## Rules
- Customer explicitly accepts required texts at checkout; accepted version stored with the order.
- Legal texts versioned; old orders keep their version.
- Commercial e-mail/SMS only with recorded consent (TR: İYS); confirm with expert.
