# Tax, Returns and Compliance (Turkey)

> Not legal or tax advice. Checked against law-firm/integrator summaries on the verified date; confirm with an expert.

## Registration and seller info
- Register the site/app in **ETBİS** (the Ministry of Trade's e-commerce information system) before selling.
- Show trade name, MERSİS number (central trade registry), tax office and number, address, phone, e-mail, KEP address (registered e-mail, if any).

## Required texts
Pre-information form (incl. mediation/arbitration info) · distance sales contract · return/withdrawal policy · KVKK privacy notice · privacy and cookie policies · membership agreement · terms of use. Tailor them; never copy another site's.
- Shown and accepted before order completion; accepted version stored with the order. Texts versioned.

## Withdrawal and returns
- **14 days** without reason; for goods it starts at delivery.
- 2026 amendment (Official Gazette "Resmî Gazete", RG 24.05.2025 no. 32909, in force **1 Jan 2026**): no return-shipping charge to the consumer for withdrawal via the carrier named in the pre-information form; previously exempt devices (phones, tablets, computers, smartwatches) are now within withdrawal scope — **verify the exact exceptions in the official text**.
- Record reason, dates and refund amount per order; refunds back to the original payment method within the legal period.

## Tax and invoices
- Tax rates are configuration, never hard-coded. Prices tax-inclusive; breakdown per line.
- e-Arşiv (e-archive invoice) / e-Fatura (e-invoice) via an approved integrator. From 2026 e-Arşiv is the general rule; small paper-invoice allowances end 31 Dec 2026 — confirm your obligations with the accountant.

## Marketing messages
Commercial SMS/e-mail/calls only with recorded consent registered in **İYS** (the national message-consent registry); opt-out in every message.
