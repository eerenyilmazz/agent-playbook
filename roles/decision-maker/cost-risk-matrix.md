# Cost-Risk Matrix

## Score options (1 low – 5 high, one line of reasoning each)
Build cost · run cost · risk · irreversibility · time · value.

| Value \ Risk | Low risk | High risk |
|---|---|---|
| **High value** | Do it | Do it in small steps, flagged, with rollback |
| **Low value** | Only if cheap | Don't |

## Risk level = likelihood × impact
| | Low impact | Medium | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| **High** | Medium | High | Critical |
| **Medium** | Low | Medium | High |
| **Low** | Low | Low | Medium |

- **Critical:** mitigate before work starts.
- **High:** owner + plan + watcher.
- **Medium/Low:** record and monitor.
